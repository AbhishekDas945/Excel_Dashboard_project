__<h1> Indian E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel) </h1>__

## __Project Overview__

This project features an end-to-end interactive Indian E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It transforms raw transactional e-commerce data from 2023 to 2025 into actionable business intelligence, enabling stakeholders to monitor revenue growth, order fulfillment efficiency, customer retention, and regional performance across 15 Indian states.

## **Dashboard Features & Visualizations**

- **Dynamic Slicers & Filtering**: Users can dynamically filter the entire dashboard by Year (2023, 2024, 2025) and State (Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal).   
Distribution Status (Pie Chart): Visualizes order fulfillment efficiency across five stages: Delivered (80%), Cancelled (8%), Returned (7%), Failed (3%), and Processing (2%).  

- **New vs. Repeat Customer (Donut Chart)**: Tracks customer loyalty by comparing Repeat Customers (83%) against New Customers (17%).  

- **Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)**: Maps month-over-month revenue fluctuations from January to December to highlight seasonal demand peaks.  

- **Top 10 Categories by Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)**: Ranks product categories by revenue generation, comparing Electronics, Appliances, Home & Kitchen, Furniture, Fashion, Footwear, Bags, Health & Wellness, Sports, and Toys.  

- **Payment Method Breakdown (Column Chart)**: Analyzes customer payment preferences across UPI, Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, Wallet, EMI, Pay Later, Gift Card, and Cardless EMI. 

## 💡 **Key Business Insights**

- **Q4 Festive Season Surge**: Monthly sales grow steadily from January to September (~₹130M–₹195M) before experiencing a sharp upward spike between October and December, peaking at over ₹350M in December.  

- **Category Dominance**: Electronics is the primary revenue driver (generating over ₹1,200M in sales), followed by Appliances as the second-largest category (~₹400M), while categories like Home & Kitchen, Furniture, and Fashion contribute moderate volumes.   

- **Strong Customer Retention**: Repeat buyers account for 83% of the customer base, demonstrating high platform stickiness and repeat purchasing behavior.  

- **Order Fulfillment & Return Rate**: While 80% of orders are successfully delivered, 15% of total orders are lost to cancellations (8%) and returns (7%), highlighting an area for logistics and quality assurance optimization.  

- **Digital Payment Adoption**: Digital transaction modes lead order volume, followed closely by Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, and Debit Card transactions. 

## 💻 **Tools & Excel Skills Applied**

- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**: Structured raw data into clean Excel Tables for dynamic range updates.

- **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**: Aggregated multi-dimensional data to power KPI cards and visual trends. 

- **Interactive Slicers**: Connected report connections across multiple Pivot Tables for seamless single-click filtering by Year and State. 

- **Custom UI & Visual Design**: Applied custom formatting, modern iconography, and cohesive color themes to create an executive-ready layout.
